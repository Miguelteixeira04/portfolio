ONEIRIC

Oneiric is a single-player, first-person exploration game that simulates a journey through the human psyche. Developed in Unreal Engine, the project challenges players to navigate non-linear spaces to reconstruct fragmented memories, utilizing advanced telemetry and contextual AI dialogue.

The Conscious Workspace

Level 1 - The stable conscious baseline represented as a minimalist room structure.

Procedural Garden of Voices

Level 2 - Subconscious depth featuring constructive layouts that randomize environmental elements and fog during runtime.

Abstract Memory Space

Level 3 - Non-linear core structure where the player interacts with dynamic artifacts to trigger sensemaking mechanics.
