SPACE INVADERS 3D

I was responsible for the full 3D development of this game, built entirely with Three.js and vanilla JavaScript. Every invader, barrier and spaceship was modelled procedurally out of individual voxel cubes, generated directly from pixel-art matrices.

This project reimagines the classic arcade title as a fully playable 3D remake, featuring three distinct invader types (red, yellow and pink), destructible green barriers, and a dual camera system that lets players switch between perspective and orthographic views in real time.

The game includes a complete progression loop, tracking score, lives and level difficulty, alongside a persistent high-score system. A full pause menu preserves the exact game state — including active invaders and camera mode — via localStorage, allowing players to resume exactly where they left off.

I also designed the surrounding UI: an animated starfield menu, victory and game-over screens with dedicated sound effects, and a dedicated sound settings panel allowing independent control over music, effects and ambient audio.
