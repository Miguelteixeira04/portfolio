Bartle Z

I was responsible for the front-end development and responsive interfaces using JavaScript and HTML/CSS. Furthermore, I managed data integration and conducted extensive usability testing to ensure cross-device consistency.

This project introduces a practical approach to Bartle’s Taxonomy. Instead of relying on traditional questionnaires, the game assesses psychological profiles (such as Killers or Achievers) through real-time decision-making and in-game behaviour.

The system seamlessly automates data collection via a REST API. The gameplay confronts users with ethical dilemmas(balancing competition against cooperation)and perception tests, analysing their choices to precisely determine player attributes.

We tested the game with nearly 60 participants, yielding highly successful results. Players were required to complete a questionnaire a week prior to establish a baseline psychological profile. The core objective was to replicate these results through our gamification process, ultimately achieving an impressive 89% precision rate.
