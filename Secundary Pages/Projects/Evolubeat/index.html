EVOLUBEAT

EvoluBeat is a tool that lets anyone compose a soundtrack for a film, no musical training required. The user describes the emotional shape of a scene and the system writes the music to match it.

At its core is an evolutionary engine. Each melody is treated as an individual in a population that mutates, recombines and is scored against the desired emotion. Across successive generations, the music evolves toward the feeling the creator is looking for.

Instead of a single fixed output, EvoluBeat offers a living process: the user can keep evolving, steer the direction, and select the take that best fits their emotional keyframes.

How It Works

From feeling to finished cue in three movements.

1

Set Keyframes

Mark moments in the timeline and assign each one an emotion: joy, tension, calm, sadness or something epic.

2

Evolve

The engine breeds and mutates melodies, scoring every candidate against your keyframes generation after generation.

3

Select & Export

Audition the fittest results, nudge the direction, and export the cue that matches your scene.
