Exhibition Design

IVAN
CHERMAYEFF

An exhibition celebrating the life and language of Ivan Chermayeff, the designer who turned bold shape, primary colour and playful collage into one of the most recognisable visual vocabularies of the twentieth century. Six rooms, six chapters of his world.

The Exhibition

Visitors move through six themed rooms, each translating a facet of Chermayeff's practice into a spatial experience.

01

Who Was Ivan Chermayeff?

This opening room tells the story of the designer, illustrator and collagist behind a century of unforgettable marks.

02

The Marks I

An entry hall of iconic identities. From NBC to Mobil and Chase, the logos that made Chermayeff & Geismar a household language.

03

The Marks II

More marks, and the thinking behind them. A second wall of identities showing the range of a single, restless visual language.

04

Play the Marks

An interactive wall where the logos become a game. Visitors build, test and play with the marks in three dimensions.

05

Collapse

A room devoted to collage. Torn paper, found objects and photography layered with a child's freedom and a master's intent.

06

Make Your Own

The closing room turns collage into interaction. Projection and live tools invite visitors to become collagists themselves, and leave with a piece of their own.

Communication

The exhibition's visual language beyond the gallery walls: posters, tickets, street presence and social media.
